About

I'm a software engineer in Orange County, California, and I like owning a product from end to end. For the last three years I've been the only engineer on CircleRanks, a movie and TV rating app with 54,000 users and about 15,000 active each month. That means the Flutter app on iOS and Android, the Node.js backend, the Firestore data model, a self-hosted search server, the data pipelines, and the release process around all of it.

The work I enjoy most is the kind with a real constraint: a similarity engine that can't expose anyone's ratings, a data pipeline on a fixed API budget, or a burst of events that has to land safely.

Before CircleRanks I interned at IMVU twice and at Transparent Sky. Outside work I build things I want to use, like a job-application autofill extension and a native iPhone app for tracking a newborn's day. I'm looking for a senior mobile or full-stack role. You can reach me at anthonychavez1337@gmail.com.
